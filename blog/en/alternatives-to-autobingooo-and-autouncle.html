Alternatives to AutoBINGOOO and AutoUncle: what each tool covers for dealers

A dealer who wants to watch listings from several portals in one place has four kinds of tool to look at: AutoBINGOOO and SourcAuto are built for dealers, AutoUncle compares prices for buyers, and WeltScout watches up to 29 portals in 14 countries and alerts on WhatsApp. WeltScout costs more than both dealer tools, checks once or twice a day (more often only with an add-on by agreement) and does not rate prices. The facts about competitors come from their own pages, read on 28 October 2026, and we have not tested them. WeltScout wrote this comparison.

Which tool is for what

AutoBINGOOO presents itself as a search engine for dealers, vehicle assessors, auction houses and leasing companies and claims 13 countries, 35+ portals and more than 10 million listings. SourcAuto says it is built for dealers and brokers and monitors 25+ sources across 10 EU countries. AutoUncle, as described in search results, compares used-car prices in 14 European markets and rates single listings against comparable cars (read on 28 October 2026).

Where WeltScout is weaker

Against the competitors we read, WeltScout is weaker on price, check frequency and price assessment. Its cheapest plan, Business, starts at €129.90 per user per month excluding VAT, against €45 including VAT at AutoBINGOOO; SourcAuto checks every 3 to 12 hours while WeltScout checks once or twice a day; and WeltScout does not rate vehicle prices.

  • Price. Compare WeltScout (from €129.90 excluding VAT per user per month) with €45 and €62 a month at AutoBINGOOO (including VAT) and €319 to €1,639 a year at SourcAuto (excluding VAT).
  • Frequency. WeltScout checks for new listings once (Business) or twice (Business +) a day; more frequent checks are an add-on by agreement. An alert therefore does not arrive the moment a listing is posted.
  • Price assessment. WeltScout finds the listing and shows the price from the ad; whether the price is good, you decide. AutoBINGOOO Pro and AutoUncle offer assessment.
  • App language. The app is in English for now; support answers in Slovenian, Croatian, Serbian, English, German, Dutch and French.
  • Switzerland. AutoBINGOOO names Switzerland among its countries; WeltScout's list of 14 countries does not include it.

Where WeltScout can be the better choice

WeltScout's list of countries includes Slovenia and Croatia, while AutoBINGOOO's page names no Western Balkan country among its countries (it adds "many more"), so a dealer who also searches Slovenian and Croatian portals should check that point with each tool.

  • WhatsApp alerts in every plan; SourcAuto names e-mail and AutoBINGOOO names no channel.
  • Searches per customer or for your own stock; each customer gets a search with a name, phone and e-mail.
  • Support in seven languages, including Slovenian and Croatian.
  • A 7-day trial without a card.

The countries WeltScout covers: Slovenia, Croatia, Austria, Germany, Italy, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Spain, Portugal, Poland, Romania and Sweden. The list of portals per country is on the home page.

Frequently asked questions

What is an alternative to AutoBINGOOO?

For dealers, SourcAuto (25+ sources in 10 EU countries) and WeltScout (up to 29 portals in 14 countries) are comparable by their own pages. AutoUncle is, by its description, more a price comparison for buyers. Check each tool's coverage for your countries.

Is WeltScout cheaper than AutoBINGOOO?

No. WeltScout costs from €129.90 per user per month excluding VAT, AutoBINGOOO €45 or €62 a month including VAT (vendor pages, read on 28 October 2026).

Does WeltScout send alerts the moment a listing is posted?

No. It checks for new listings once or twice a day depending on the plan; more frequent checks are an add-on by agreement.

Does WeltScout rate whether a car's price is good?

No. It shows the price from the listing; you decide.
