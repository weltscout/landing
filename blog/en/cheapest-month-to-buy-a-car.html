Cheapest month to buy a car: is there one?

We found no verifiable data that points to one cheapest month for buying a car. Prices do shift with sellers' targets and new model years, but the effect depends on the seller and the model, so we advise buyers, and dealers sourcing for customers, to track the market for their own model. As of October 2026.

Does a cheapest month exist

We cannot prove one. In a short review of public sources on 19 October 2026 we found no statistic showing that any month is the cheapest for buying a car. Claims about December, January or September that appear in dealer and portal advice are not backed by data we could check, so we do not state them as fact.

In the UK, the plate change is the one fixed date

The age identifier on a UK number plate changes twice a year, in March and September, according to the DVLA leaflet INF104, which we read on 19 October 2026. That is a fixed point in the calendar, which is why many UK guides mention those two months. We found no published data showing what it does to used-car prices, so treat it as a reason to look, not as a rule.

How to find the cheapest time for your own model

Tracking the market yourself for a few weeks shows you what is happening for your model.

  1. Pick one or two models and fix the year, engine and trim.
  2. Save the search with alerts, or repeat it on the same day each week.
  3. Each week, note the number of listings, the lowest price and the price of a typical listing in a sheet.
  4. Note which listings disappeared and after how many days.
  5. After a few weeks, compare: did supply grow or shrink, and did prices move?
  6. Decide by what the market shows, not by the calendar.

When waiting does not pay

  • You have found a car that meets every criterion, and the price is within your limit.
  • You need the car now, for work or family.
  • After a few weeks your sheet shows no fall in prices.

Someone else may buy a good car at a fair price before the supposedly cheaper month arrives.

Questions and answers

Is December really the cheapest month to buy a car?

We cannot confirm it. Year-end targets are a plausible reason for discounts, but we found no verifiable data for it. Test it with the sheet for your own model.

Is it worth buying a used car in winter?

Demand for some types of car may change with the season, convertibles for example, but it is not proven that buying in winter is cheaper. Base the decision on the supply for your model.

Is a new car cheaper at the end of the year?

New-car dealers may have annual targets, so negotiating at the end of a period is reasonable. We cannot give the size of a discount because we have no verifiable data; ask several dealers for a quote.

How long should I wait for a lower price?

There is no general rule. If after a few weeks of tracking the prices for your model have not come down, waiting is pointless.
