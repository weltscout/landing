Are car sales declining in Europe? What the 2026 registration data shows

For new cars, no: EU new car registrations were 5.3% higher in January to August 2026 than a year earlier (ACEA, 24 September 2026). The used market looks weaker in Germany, where car ownership transfers were 2.9% lower in January to September (KBA, 5 October 2026). This article is for dealers and buyers and uses only official registration statistics and one dated industry release. As of October 2026.

Are new car sales falling in the EU?

No. ACEA, the European carmakers' association, counted 7,546,397 new passenger cars registered in the EU from January to August 2026, 5.3% more than the 7,167,982 a year earlier. Across the EU, EFTA and the UK the rise was 5.8%. In August alone EU registrations were 708,211, up 4.5%. ACEA calls the data for the current year provisional and describes the growth as coming against a backdrop of rising energy prices and persistent geopolitical uncertainty.

How is the mix of powertrains changing?

The total is rising while petrol and diesel fall. Battery-electric cars took 21.7% of EU registrations in January to August 2026, against 15.8% a year earlier, and petrol and diesel together fell to 29% from 37.5%. Hybrid-electric cars lead with 36.6% (ACEA).

What is happening in the used car market?

Used-car data is thinner than new-car data, and it points in different directions by country. Germany has the clearest official series: the Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt reports ownership transfers of cars, which measure trade in used cars within the country. Slovenia and Croatia publish first registrations, which for cars that are not new mostly mean vehicles previously registered abroad.

How to read vehicle sales statistics: a checklist

  • A registration counts a vehicle entered in a register. It says nothing about the money earned.
  • Separate new and used. Ownership transfers measure trade in used cars inside a country, while first registrations of used cars mostly measure imports.
  • Compare equal periods, such as January to August against the previous January to August.
  • Check whether the data is provisional (ACEA) or final.
  • Do not add figures from sources with different definitions.
  • Remember that these statistics contain no prices.

What the data does not tell you

It does not say whether used-car prices are rising or falling, it shows no long-term trend before 2025, and it does not split imports by country of origin. We compare only the first eight or nine months of 2026 with the same months of 2025. ACEA's calendar lists the September 2026 release for 22 October 2026 and the October release for 24 November 2026.

Official sources and reading dates

  • ACEA: new car registrations, August 2026 year-to-date, published 24 September 2026.
  • KBA: vehicle registrations in September 2026 (in German), published 5 October 2026.
  • Statistical Office of Slovenia: first registrations of road vehicles, data to August 2026, read on 19 October 2026.
  • CVH's first-half 2026 release, read via tportal (Hina, 24 July 2026).

Frequently asked questions

Are car sales falling in 2026?

New car registrations are not: they were 5.3% higher in the EU in January to August 2026 (ACEA). Used-car ownership transfers in Germany are 2.9% lower to September (KBA).

Are electric car sales falling in Europe?

Not in the EU in 2026: 1,641,333 new battery-electric cars were registered in January to August, a 21.7% market share (ACEA, 24 September 2026).

Are petrol and diesel sales declining?

Yes. In the EU petrol registrations fell 18.6% and diesel 18.6% in January to August 2026, and together they hold 29% of the market (ACEA).

When will new figures appear?

ACEA's calendar lists the September 2026 release for 22 October 2026. The KBA reports monthly and published its September figures on 5 October 2026.
