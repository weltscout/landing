Buy a used car from Germany and import it to Croatia: PPMV, VAT, plates

A used car from Germany reaches Croatia without customs duty, but you pay the special tax on motor vehicles (PPMV) to the Customs Administration, and VAT depends on who sells and who buys. The tax return on form PP-MV is due within 15 days of the car entering Croatia, the tax within 15 days of the decision, and without proof of payment the car cannot be registered. The PPMV amount depends on the CO2 value, the price of the new car in Croatia and the date of first registration; the Customs Administration publishes a calculator. Status: 28 October 2026.

What you pay when you bring a used car into Croatia

Every buyer pays PPMV, whether a private person or a business (Croatian Customs Administration, notice of 16 April 2026, read on 28 October 2026). VAT of 25% on the acquisition is paid by a Croatian VAT-registered buyer who buys from a VAT-registered seller in the EU; a private person pays no Croatian VAT, because the seller accounts for VAT under its own country's rules, and with a margin-scheme dealer there is no Croatian VAT at all (gov.hr, read on 28 October 2026).

For PPMV a used car's tax is the remainder of the tax that would have been due at first registration as new, reduced by a percentage that depends on the time since first registration. The Customs Administration's own example: a new petrol car of 35,000 euros with 137 g/km CO2 would carry 1,093.59 euros; after 14 months the remainder is 1,093.59 x 74.12% = 810.57 euros. This is an official example, not an estimate for your car; use the calculator, which asks for the price of the car as new in Croatia, not the market price.

Plates for the drive home

You need plates and insurance that are valid in every country on your route; temporary plates are not harmonised in the EU (Your Europe). A German export plate is valid for the period of the insurance, at most one year (§ 45 FZV), a short-term plate until the end of the fifth day after issue (§ 42 FZV). As one example the Berlin office charges at least 33.20 euros for the export plate plus the plates (Service Berlin).

What this means for buyers and dealers

For a private buyer

Count the 15 days from the day the car entered Croatia, not from the purchase. Get the COC and an invoice with a VAT or margin note before you pay.

For a dealer

Know before each purchase whether you buy from a taxpayer or a margin dealer: the forms and documents depend on it. Frequently asked questions

How much is PPMV on a used car from Germany?

It depends on CO2, the price of the new car in Croatia and the time since first registration. The Customs Administration's official example gives 810.57 euros for a 14-month-old car; for your car use its informative calculator.

How long do I have to file the PPMV return?

At most 15 days from the day the vehicle enters Croatia; the tax is payable within 15 days of the decision (Customs Administration, notice of 16 April 2026).

Do I pay Croatian VAT as a private person?

No, when you buy from a VAT-registered dealer: the seller accounts for VAT under its own rules (gov.hr). Buying from a private seller can involve an administrative fee; confirm the name and amount with the Tax Administration.

Can I register before paying PPMV?

No. Without proof of payment and notice that collection is complete, the car cannot be registered at a technical inspection station.
