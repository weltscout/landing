Most sought-after used cars: what the official figures show

In the UK, the Ford Fiesta was the most traded used car in 2025 with 303,090 transactions, ahead of the Vauxhall Corsa (247,853) and the Volkswagen Golf (226,082), according to SMMT figures published on 10 February 2026. The UK is the only market we checked where an official or trade-body source publishes a model ranking. This article is for dealers sourcing stock and for buyers comparing markets. As of October 2026.

What "sought-after" means and what can be measured

Searching and buying are different data points, and official statistics count only registrations and ownership changes. Three things can be measured:

  • Searches: held by advertising portals and search engines. The official sources we read do not contain them.
  • Ownership changes or transactions: published by the UK, Germany and Austria.
  • First registrations: published by Slovenia and Croatia, with new and used cars shown separately in Slovenia.

United Kingdom: the 2025 and 2026 figures

SMMT counted 7,807,872 used car transactions in 2025, up 2.2%, with the top five models shown below (SMMT, 10 February 2026). In the second quarter of 2026 it counted 2,009,318 transactions, up 0.7% on the same quarter of 2025 (SMMT used car sales page, read 19 October 2026).

Which trends the sources support

  • Used electric and hybrid cars are growing: in the UK, 274,815 used battery-electric cars changed hands in 2025 (+45.7%) and 407,531 hybrids (+28.6%) (SMMT, 10 February 2026). In Austria, used electric registrations reached 34,700 (+20.9%, a 4.2% share) while diesel fell 5.8% (Statistik Austria, 14 January 2026).
  • Germany is weaker in 2026: ownership changes of passenger cars from January to September 2026 were 2.9% lower than a year earlier (KBA, 5 October 2026).
  • Slovenia leans towards new cars: first registrations of used passenger cars fell 4% in 2025 while new ones rose 8% (SURS).

The official sources we reviewed contain no figure for the idea that buyers look for fuel-efficient models because of fuel prices, or for the idea that more people import from abroad, so we do not present them as trends.

How to read market statistics: five questions

  1. What is counted: a first registration, an ownership change or a transaction?
  2. Which period does it cover and when was it published?
  3. Are passenger cars the only vehicles included?
  4. Is the figure provisional or final?
  5. Does it say anything about models, or only about the total?

Frequently asked questions

What are the most sought-after used cars in the UK?

By transactions in 2025, the Ford Fiesta, Vauxhall Corsa and Volkswagen Golf led, followed by the Ford Focus and BMW 3 Series (SMMT, 10 February 2026). That measures cars sold, not searches.

Is there an official ranking for Germany, Austria, Slovenia or Croatia?

In the official publications we read (KBA, Statistik Austria, SURS, DZS), none contains a ranking of used models. They give totals, and for Germany and Austria also breakdowns by fuel type.

Can I compare figures between countries?

Not directly, because the countries count different things. Compare directions of change.

Is the used car market growing?

It depends on the country and period. UK transactions grew 2.2% in 2025, while German ownership changes were 2.9% lower from January to September 2026 than a year earlier.
