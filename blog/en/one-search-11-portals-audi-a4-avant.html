Limits of the measurement

This is one search, run on one day with a test account. On another day or with different filters the numbers will differ, because listings are added and removed. Below are four limits to keep in mind when you read the tables.

  • Filters. Ten filters narrow the selection, for example a price up to €25,000 and dealers only. A portal's share in this search does not say which portal has more cars overall.
  • Zeros. Zero matches means the app returned none with these filters, not that the portal has no such cars.
  • Prices. The prices come from the listings. WeltScout does not inspect vehicles or check their history, so a price says nothing about a car's condition.
  • Four examples. Four listings are not a sample and do not allow a price comparison by country.
