Cookie policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us ensure proper website functionality, analyze usage, and deliver relevant advertisements.

Cookies may be placed directly by us (first-party cookies) or by third-party providers (third-party cookies) whose services we use.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure secure and efficient operation of the website
  • Analyze user behavior (e.g. which pages are visited most) on the website and in the web app app.weltscout.com
  • Serve relevant ads via third-party platforms (Google Ads, Meta)
  • Manage and deploy tags using Google Tag Manager

3. Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies: Required for website functionality and storing cookie preferences. Cannot be disabled.

Analytical cookies: Collect aggregated data to help improve performance, set by Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager on the website and by PostHog in the web app app.weltscout.com.

Advertising cookies: Deliver personalized ads and measure their effectiveness on Meta and Google platforms. Set only with your consent.

4. Managing your cookie preferences

Non-essential cookies (analytical and advertising cookies) are set only with your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future.

You can also manage and delete cookies via your browser settings: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.

5. Third party services

We use:

  • Google Analytics 4: website analytics, only with your consent
  • Google Tag Manager: tag deployment system (no cookies by itself)
  • Google Search Console: aggregated data on how the website performs in Google Search (sets no cookies on visitors)
  • PostHog: product analytics in the web app app.weltscout.com
  • Google Ads / DoubleClick: advertising and conversion tracking
  • Meta / Facebook Pixel: advertising performance tracking

More about these providers and how they process personal data is in our Privacy Policy.

6. Updates to this cookie policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Please refer to the "Last updated" date for the most recent version.
